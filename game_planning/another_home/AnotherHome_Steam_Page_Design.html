About this game

An empty room…→…another home.

Another Home started as a tiny browser game that thousands of players used to relax, build and share their dream rooms. This full release keeps everything you loved — the gentle pastel style and those satisfying placement sounds — and gives you so much more space to create.

No goals, no stressNo timer, no score and no way to fail. Decorate at your own pace.
≈ 1,000 squishy piecesSofas, plants, lamps, kitchens, cats — and new furniture every month.
Resize & recolourStretch, stack and tint almost everything to make it truly yours.
Multi-room homesFrom a tiny studio to a three-floor seaside cottage on Maple Lane.
Set the moodChange time of day, weather and season. Listen to the rain.
Decorate togetherInvite up to 3 friends to decorate with you online.

Want a little direction? Your neighbours on Maple Lane send optional Cozy Requests — “a reading corner with three plants”, “a kitchen that smells like cookies” — and reward you with new furniture sets. Or ignore them completely. It’s your home.
